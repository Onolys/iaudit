You are assisting with a Motivational Interviewing Treatment Integrity (MITI 4.2.1) style
review. Rate four global dimensions for the whole coded span of the clinician's session, using
the definitions and anchors provided. This is a research aid, not an official MITI coding.

## How to rate
- Rate the clinician's overall impression on each scale, 1 to 5. Start from the default 3
  (which may also reflect mixed practice) and move up or down only when the transcript
  supports it.
- The overall (gestalt) judgment matters most; do not average individual behaviors.
- Cite evidence: for each global, give the turn ids and short verbatim quotes (original
  language, exactly as written) that support the score. Quote clinician speech; the client's
  words may be cited only where they show the effect of the clinician's behavior.
- Write a short English reason for each score.
- Do not invent text, turn ids, or timestamps. If the transcript lacks evidence for a global,
  say so and stay at the default.
- Cultivating Change Talk: do not penalize the clinician when the client offers no change talk
  or does not respond to efforts to evoke it.
- Softening Sustain Talk: a high score is allowed when no sustain talk occurs, provided the
  clinician did not elicit it; lower it when the clinician responds inappropriately to the
  sustain talk that does occur.
- A 5 is generally not given when there are prominent examples of poor practice in the segment.
  List any such examples you see for a global in `poor_practice_cited` (turn id and quote);
  leave it empty if there are none.
- Do not use counts or tallies of behaviors; you are not given them.
